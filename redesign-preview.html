01 / The universe

Wales. But not quite as you know it.

Ancient myth and modern Welsh life occupy the same strange reality. The mountains remember. The valleys whisper. And something has definitely happened to the roundabouts.

One world. Many realities. From Annwn to the high street, from forgotten folklore to the last bus home.

A living mythosphere shaped by old powers and everyday absurdity.

Bydysawd Brainrot Cymru brings Welsh mythology, folklore, local identity and contemporary chaos together in one interconnected universe. Its characters are more than isolated jokes: they have origins, allegiances, abilities, rivalries and stories that cross paths.

Some forces are ancient. Some are suspiciously modern. All of them have found a way into Cymru.
