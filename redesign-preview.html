A little about us

The people behind the universe.

Bydysawd is built around the worlds, characters and stories. Here's a little about the creative studio behind it — and where the journey began.

Created by Vizard Comms.A creative studio developing original worlds, characters, collectible ideas and connected stories.

One universe. A creative vision.

Vizard Comms is the independent creative business behind Bydysawd, guiding its development, storytelling and future growth.

Bydysawd Brainrot Cymru was the original project identity — the starting point from which the wider universe has grown. Its characters and creative history remain part of the story.

01 / The master universe

More than a world. A universe of its own.

Bydysawd is the home of connected worlds, original characters, collectible creations and stories that cross boundaries. Each world has its own identity. Together, they form something much bigger.

One universe. Many worlds. Each world brings its own mythology, characters, places, conflicts and stories.

A connected universe built for discovery.

Every world within Bydysawd has room for its own legends, characters, cultures and creative expression. Stories can stand alone, cross over and reveal new connections as the universe grows.

We begin in Cymru — not because the universe ends there, but because every great universe needs somewhere to begin.
