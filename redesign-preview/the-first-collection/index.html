The beginning of the collection

A whole world in every card.

The First Collection brings together the original characters of Bydysawd Cymru — from ancient mythic forces and otherworldly beings to modern Welsh oddities, local legends and unexpected heroes.

Each character belongs to a wider world of factions, histories, rivalries and strange connections. The cards are an invitation to explore that world, one discovery at a time.

The First Collection refers to the 39 original Set One characters. Y Storiwr, Card 040, remains part of the expanding living roster rather than the original 39-card count.
