Seven powers · One unstable universe

The great factions

The factions are not simply teams. They are competing philosophies, forces and ways of understanding reality — in a state of perpetual negotiation.

🔥

Tylwyth y Tân

Children of Fire. Industrial valleys, passion, music and controlled chaos. Deep crimson.

📜

Urdd y Mabinogi

Order of the Mabinogi. Ancient mythic bloodlines, honour, storytelling and dramatic irony. Aged gold.

🌊

Plant yr Annwn

Children of the Otherworld. Tricksters, shapeshifters and liminal beings. Forest green.

🎙️

Crachach Cosmig

The Cosmic Establishment. Unknowable power, impossible committees and ceremonial Barbour. Purple.

⚙️

Y Peiriant

Machine-born entities, corrupted technology and eternal batteries. Steel blue.

🍟

Clwb y Cyrri Sawss

The Late Night Alliance. Service-station prophets, takeaway kings and parking-lot druids. Amber.

⛰️

Yr Unfactioned

Older than the factions. Older than the war. Warm white.
