Official character dossier · Card 001

Draig Draig Draig Bale-ino

The Roundabout Dragon

He has occupied the Cwmbran roundabout since 1982. The road was built around him. The golf course remains a separate matter.

Tylwyth y TânSporting Specter DragonSet One · Origin Card
01 · The beginning

Origin story

An ancient entity from Annwn, Draig Draig Draig Bale-ino fought as the red dragon at Dinas Emrys before eventually settling into a rather more permanent position at a Cwmbran roundabout in 1982.

The dual carriageway was built around him. He has never considered this an inconvenience. During the Forbidden Broadcast of 1984, all six eyes opened at once — and the ordinary world became considerably less ordinary.

Three heads. Three opinions. One roundabout. The football-shirt head is matter-of-fact, the backwards golf-cap head carries an air of smug authority, and the third appears serenely unbothered by the collapse of normal reality.

02 · Capabilities

Power & weakness

Mythical football skill: he can defeat opponents through extraordinary football ability, with the added tactical advantage of teleporting to golf courses mid-combat.

The par-three problem: if a par 3 golf hole is nearby, he immediately disengages. No explanation has been offered. None appears likely.

03 · Recorded words

Voice line

“Ymlaen… after this hole.”

04 · Connections

Story threads

The Master Origin Canon links Draig Draig Draig Bale-ino with Barti Marcs yr Mwg Du. The precise nature of their connection remains unresolved — and neither party appears particularly keen to clarify matters.

05 · Canon integrity

What is established?

Draig’s faction, archetype, territory, powers, weakness, voice line and card statistics are recorded in the Set One source. His ancient Annwn origin, Dinas Emrys history, 1982 arrival and the 1984 Forbidden Broadcast are part of the wider universe canon. The exact Barti favour remains deliberately unresolved.
