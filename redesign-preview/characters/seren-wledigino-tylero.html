Seren Wledigino Tylero

The Eclipse Rider

She has been riding towards the darkness for years. The darkness is beginning to move out of her way.

01 · The beginning

Origin story

A warrior woman of the 1980s rock archetype rides a white horse along a vast beach beneath a total eclipse. Golden sound waves follow every hoofbeat. She carries a glass of red wine and the absolute conviction that she is heading in the right direction.

02 · Capabilities

Power & weakness

Power: Bends light through voice resonance.

Weakness: Needs a hero who never arrives.

03 · Recorded words

Voice line

“Turn around… apocalypse.”

04 · Story threads

Connections & curiosities

The eclipse rider's path intersects the wider Y Nos Hiraf story. She is always moving towards something the horizon has not yet revealed.

05 · Canon integrity

What is established?

Card 007 is confirmed in the canonical roster. The eclipse imagery and Rhossili Bay setting belong to the character's fiction. Her later Y Nos Hiraf connections should be read alongside the developing seasonal storyline, not as Welsh historical tradition.
