The concept

Part podcast. Part broadcast from the Otherworld.

Radio Annwn exists in two places at once: a genuine Bydysawd podcast for listeners, and a mysterious station within the world itself. Episodes can move between creator-led conversation, rotating in-universe presenters, character dispatches and specially produced story transmissions.

The sound

Balanced between strange and familiar.

Atmospheric and cinematic, but never so serious that the Welsh absurdity disappears. Radio Annwn should feel like a station you have accidentally tuned into — then keep listening to because the signal knows more than it should.

Presenters

A rotating voice roster.

There is no single permanent host. Different episodes can be fronted by different voices from the universe, with a consistent station identity holding the format together.

Gelert Geraldino is a natural podcast voice within the world: a thoughtful, quietly probing interviewer whose instinct is to listen carefully.

Episode formats

One station. Several kinds of transmission.

Studio conversations — creator-led discussion, lore and behind-the-scenes making of Bydysawd.

Dispatches from Annwn — short in-universe reports, strange sightings and messages from the Llyfr Braint Annwn.

Character transmissions — interviews, monologues and encounters with the people and entities of Cymru.

Story specials — audio chapters, seasonal broadcasts and narrative events such as Y Nos Hiraf.
