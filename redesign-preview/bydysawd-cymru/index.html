01 · The nature of this world

A familiar place. An unfamiliar reality.

Bydysawd Cymru is a living world where Cymru, Annwn and the mortal world have existed in uneasy balance since time immemorial. Its landscapes and everyday life are recognisably Welsh, but the ordinary rules do not always hold. Language can become operational. Food can become mythological. Transport can develop agency. A local landmark may have been ancient long before anyone thought to ask.

Welsh mythology, folklore, language, places and community life are the foundations of the atmosphere. Original characters, events and supernatural explanations make this a new fictional universe — not a claim that its invented stories are traditional Welsh folklore.

The humour looks outward: at misunderstanding, bureaucracy, overconfidence and the absurdity of the wider world. Welsh language and culture are never the punchline.

02 · The realms

Three realities. One uneasy balance.

The oldest record describes a relationship between three realms. Their boundaries have never been as settled as the authorities would like people to believe.

Realm 01

Cymru

The living Welsh world: its communities, coasts, mountains, towns, roads and everyday places. The familiar ground where the extraordinary keeps turning up.

Realm 02

Annwn

The Otherworld of Welsh mythic imagination. A realm of ancient powers, strange presences and stories that do not always stay on their own side of the boundary.

Realm 03

The Mortal World

The wider human reality beyond the mythic balance. Its relationship with Cymru and Annwn forms part of the oldest account — and remains deliberately mysterious.

These are broad world-building descriptions. The exact geography, mechanics and history of the realms remain open for future canon development.

03 · A history of things becoming stranger

The timeline of the Myth Singularity.

The official record is incomplete. The following milestones are established in the Master Origin Canon.

Before time

Time Immemorial

Annwn, Cymru and the mortal world exist in uneasy balance.

Age of Legend

The red dragon at Dinas Emrys

Draig Draig Draig Bale-ino fights as the red dragon, then retires — not in defeat, in principle.

1982

The Cwmbran roundabout settlement

The dragon takes up residence at a roundabout. The dual carriageway is built around him. This arrangement continues.

1984

The Forbidden Broadcast

During a broadcast on S4C, all six of the dragon's eyes open. Y Cyngor forms in response. The Brainrot Age begins.

1989

The Gower Convergence

Muninn, raven of memory, becomes Cigfran y Rave and remains behind in a Welsh field after the Convergence.

11 Aug 1999

The Great Eclipse

The boundary weakens permanently. Seren Wledigino Tylero arrives in Cymru.

1999 onward

The acceleration

Language becomes operational, food becomes mythological and transport gains agency. The extraordinary begins to settle into daily life.

2019

The book outside Pontypool Market

The Llyfr Braint Annwn is found in a carrier bag outside Pontypool Market. It records 39 entities.

Now

The Myth Singularity Era

The roster continues to grow. The old record is no longer large enough to contain the world it describes.

05 · The record

The book is not wrong. It is outdated.

The oldest public account of the modern era comes from a book that should probably have been kept somewhere drier.

“As recorded in the Llyfr Braint Annwn — the Book of Entitlements of the Otherworld — found in a carrier bag outside Pontypool market, 2019. Condition: damp. Contents: true.

The Book is not wrong. The Book is outdated. This is not the same thing.”

Llyfr Braint Annwn · Official framing text · Master Origin Canon

A growing archive

Thirty-nine entries. And counting.

The 2019 book records 39 entities. The original Set One collection preserves those 39 characters as its founding roster. The living roster has since expanded: Y Storiwr is officially numbered Card 040, without closing the door to further additions.

The book remains an important record, but it is not the final authority on what exists. Some stories were never written down. Some have been altered. Some are still being carried across the boundary by people who may or may not be entirely trustworthy.

Barti's true nature — rescuer, thief or inventor — remains deliberately unresolved. The record refuses to settle the matter.

06 · The unanswered question

Who gets to decide where a world ends?

There are hints that the separation between realms has a history more complicated than the official record admits. One strand of that history is reserved for future development.

Reserved development · January 2027

Y Cytundeb Saith Diwrnod

The Seven-Day Agreement was intended as a temporary arrangement between six ancestral communities. Its expiry, the Eighth Day and the ancient presence involved remain an unresolved mystery.

Unresolved

The Eighth Page

Barti Marcs possesses an original witness document connected to the Eighth Day, held in the Archive of Things That Never Happened. Its full contents and meaning remain unknown.

This material is a reserved exploratory framework, not locked historical canon. The cause of the First Separation, the ancient presence's role and the identities of the six signatories remain open.

07 · Stories unfolding now

The world is not waiting for permission.

First major storyline · Seasonal saga

Y Nos Hiraf — The Longest Night

The winter solstice arrives. The sun does not rise. Across Cymru, the balance begins to shift — and an ancient winter kingdom becomes central to the fate of two worlds. This is original Bydysawd fiction, drawing inspiration from seasonal traditions and Welsh mythic atmosphere.
