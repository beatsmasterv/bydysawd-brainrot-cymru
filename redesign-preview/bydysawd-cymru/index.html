A world of its own

Wales, but the veil is wearing thin.

Bydysawd Cymru draws on the texture of Welsh life: its landscapes, language, folklore, communities and wonderfully specific everyday absurdities.

Here, the Mabinogi sits beside motorway junctions. Beings from Annwn have their own unfinished business. Cosmic forces develop administrative procedures. Nothing is too ordinary to become legendary — and nothing legendary is safe from a little Welsh chaos.

The world blends genuine Welsh cultural and mythological inspiration with original characters, events and fiction created for Bydysawd.

The forces within Cymru

Seven factions. One unsettled world.

Not everyone agrees on how this world should work. Some have ancient claims. Some have new ambitions. Others refuse to pick a side.
