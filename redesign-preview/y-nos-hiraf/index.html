The story so far

A dawn that never came.

On the longest night, the sun fails to rise. What first feels like an impossible winter becomes something far more deliberate: a struggle over a kingdom, a lost love, the power of the Hunt and the right of people to choose what happens to their world.

As the darkness deepens, old connections return to the surface. A warning is carried in song. A forbidden record waits in an impossible archive. And the last light may demand more than anyone is prepared to give.

Y Nos Hiraf is original Bydysawd fiction, drawing creative inspiration from winter folklore and seasonal traditions. The story's characters and events are fictional; genuine Welsh traditions, including the Mari Lwyd's pwnco, are treated as distinct cultural inspirations.

The heart of the story

A new dawn must be chosen.

The destination is not simply to defeat winter. The story is moving towards a different kind of resolution: a boundary made by consent, a kingdom whose people can choose their future, and a cost that cannot be undone.

Seren's eclipse power and the Heart of Dawn are part of the emerging answer. But every choice leaves something behind.
