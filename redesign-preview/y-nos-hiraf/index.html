The beginning

A night that should have ended.

On the longest night of the year, dawn never comes. The familiar world slips out of balance as ancient forces move in the darkness.

What begins as an impossible winter becomes a struggle over memory, power, love and the fragile light that remains. The story unfolds through chapters, characters and clues — with every revelation opening another door.

Y Nos Hiraf is an original Bydysawd storyline, drawing creative inspiration from winter folklore and seasonal traditions. Its characters and events belong to this fictional universe.
