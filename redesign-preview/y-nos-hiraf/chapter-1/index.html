The Last Light · The opening chapter

The final sunset slips behind the mountains. The night settles over Cymru — and then something goes wrong. Morning does not come.

Published chapter
Story file 01 / 05
“The winter solstice came. The sun did not rise.”

The first sign

At first, the darkness is mistaken for weather. The valleys wake beneath a sky that refuses to change. The clocks move. The buses, eventually, arrive. But the light does not return.

Across Cymru, ordinary flames begin to burn an unfamiliar blue. Fires that should warm a room give off a cold, spectral glow. In the distance, riders are glimpsed moving along the ridgelines — too far away to identify, too deliberate to dismiss.

The boundary between the living world and Annwn is beginning to fail. Something has disturbed the old balance, and the missing sunrise is only the first consequence.

The question left behind

If dawn has not come, who — or what — has taken it?
