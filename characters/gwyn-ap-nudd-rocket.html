Origin story

A band rehearsed in Merthyr Tydfil. The song fell apart. A drum fill sulked in the silence. Then a cowbell rang. Nobody had brought one. When Sean struck it a second time, the room opened. Five riders stood where the wall had been, their horses needing no floor. At their head was Gwyn ap Nudd, King of Annwn. He did not announce a hunt. He listened. The band found the song again, and Gwyn joined as though the rehearsal had been waiting for him. The Cŵn Annwn supplied the backing vocals. They were frightening, ancient and, to the band's lasting irritation, very good at harmonies.

Canon & continuity

Card 011 is the approved Set One identity. The Merthyr rehearsal and Cowbell of Destiny are original character mythology, drawing on the Welsh tradition of Gwyn ap Nudd and the Wild Hunt.
