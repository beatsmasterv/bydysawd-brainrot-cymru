Seren Wledigino Tylero

The Eclipse Rider

Twenty-seven years riding toward the same eclipse. To be fair, the eclipse has also been busy.

Origin story

On 11 August 1999, as the eclipse crossed Cymru, a woman saw a coastline beneath permanent darkness and understood it as instruction rather than image. When daylight returned, she was already riding west. Rhossili Bay moves beneath her like a living boundary. She is not escaping anything. She is pursuing something that keeps adjusting its distance.

Story threads & continuity

A proposed Y Nos Hiraf storyline connects Seren to Marchog y Rhew and the Fallen Winter Queen. Their mutual love and Seren’s escape through the 1999 breach are seasonal story canon in development; the final resolution remains open.

Canon notes

The master roster assigns Card 007, although the Set One source page omits the number in its heading. The Y Nos Hiraf relationship arc is proposed seasonal canon, not a fully locked master-origin capsule.
