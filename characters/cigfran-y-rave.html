Origin story

There was a field in the Gower. A sound system built by hands that had no idea what they were really building. A bassline found frequencies memory had not previously needed to have opinions about. Muninn came down to investigate, as he had investigated everything for millennia. By the time understanding arrived, it no longer seemed especially important. He returned alone that evening, as he always had — but something that should have settled back into place stayed behind. He still carries messages. He never stopped being what he is. But part of him never quite left that Welsh field at three in the morning.

Canon & continuity

Card 005 is part of the original Awakened Deck. The character’s Norse connection is explicit in the approved character story; the Welsh Gower Convergence is original Bydysawd Brainrot Cymru canon.
